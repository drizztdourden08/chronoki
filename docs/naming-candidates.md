# Project name candidates (I-1)

The aim is a name in RotP's spirit. *A Link to the Past* became *Relic of the Past*: it keeps the cadence and
swaps the hero's word for a lore word. This name should play on *Chrono Trigger* and carry a nod to its
development or its lore. During development the game was the "Dream Project", named for the "Dream Team" of
Sakaguchi, Horii and Toriyama. Like RotP, the name avoids the publisher's full mark (Chrono + Trigger together)
so the project never reads as an official product.

Names already taken in the fan scene, so we avoid them: Temporal Flux, Crimson Echoes, Resurrection,
Flames of Eternity, Jets of Time, Wings of Time, Egg of Time, Eternal Nightmare, Kajar (Engine),
Prophet's Guile, Radical Dreamers.

## The 20 candidates

| # | Name | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Retrigger** | Pulls the trigger again: the 1995 game fires once more, on PC. Also a nod to New Game+, the series' replay system. One word, easy to own. |
| 2 | **Chronoport** | "A port of Chrono", and *porta* is Latin for gate, so the name also says the Gates that carry you through time. It is the working folder's name too. |
| 3 | **Pendant of the Past** | Marle's pendant reacts to the first Gate at the Millennial Fair. The name is a sibling to *Relic of the Past*, so the two ports read as one family. |
| 4 | **Millennial Gate** | The adventure opens at the Millennial Fair (1000 AD), where the first Gate appears. Here the launcher is that gate. |
| 5 | **Epoch Trigger** | Epoch is the time machine. An epoch is also an era, and a PC's clock counts from "the epoch". It keeps the Trigger half of the title. |
| 6 | **Dream Gate** | Joins the "Dream Project" codename to the Gates. |
| 7 | **Telepod** | Lucca's machine at the fair, the device that starts the whole story. A launcher you step into. |
| 8 | **Enhasa** | The City of Dreams in the Kingdom of Zeal. A deep cut that also nods to the Dream Project. |
| 9 | **Second Dream** | The Dream Project, played a second time (New Game+). |
| 10 | **Kairos Trigger** | Greek *kairos*, "the right moment", set against *khronos*, "time". Plays on the title word for word. |
| 11 | **Dream Trigger** | Codename plus title. The fit is perfect, but *Dream Trigger 3D* (3DS, 2011) already exists. |
| 12 | **The Dream Project** | The plain codename. Generic, and hard to own. |
| 13 | **Gate Key** | The item that opens Gates on demand. A launcher is a key. |
| 14 | **Dreamstone** | The red stone of Zeal, from which the Masamune is forged. Used elsewhere (*The Dreamstone* cartoon). |
| 15 | **Echo Trigger** | A sound play on the title: the game echoing back. "Echoes" is close to *Crimson Echoes*. |
| 16 | **End of Time** | The hub between every era (Gaspar's lamp post). The launcher sits outside time and opens any era. A very common phrase. |
| 17 | **Gatelight** | The glow of an opening Gate. Short and ownable, with a softer lore tie. |
| 18 | **Nu Game+** | Nu, the series mascot, joked into New Game+. Playful but awkward to say. |
| 19 | **Trigger of the Past** | A straight mash-up with RotP. Too close to both titles. |
| 20 | **Proto Trigger** | A nod to the prototype and beta builds and to Robo's Proto Dome. A weak link to the codename. |

## Top 3 (recommended first)

1. **Chronoport.** The double meaning, a port of Chrono and a Gate, is the closest match to RotP's wit, and the
   owner already knows the name from the folder. Collision check: clear. No game, software or trademark uses it;
   the only near hits are dead CHRONOSPORT watch marks. Risk: "Chrono" alone is a common Greek root, but it is
   also half of the publisher's mark.
2. **Pendant of the Past.** It makes the RotP family explicit and rests on lore (the Pendant opens the first Gate).
   Collision check: clear. Risk: it plays on RotP more than on *Chrono Trigger* itself.
3. **Millennial Gate.** It is pure lore, the place and the moment the story starts, and it holds no part of the
   mark. Collision check: clear.

Two that dropped out of the top 3:

- **Retrigger** was the first pick before the check, but a UK trademark application, *RETRIGGER GAMES*
  (UK00004237637, July 2025), covers gaming software.
- **Epoch Trigger** has no exact match, but the word "Epoch" is crowded (Epoch Co., a Japanese game and toy maker).

The check used web searches and trademark aggregators. Before the name goes public, confirm it on USPTO TSDR and
the UK IPO search.
